Features

Everything you need to
understand your users

PulsePoint goes beyond analytics. It feels what your users feel and tells you how to fix it.

🖱
FrustrationIQ™ Detection
Detects rage clicks, inactivity drops, and hesitation patterns. Combines them into a real-time frustration score.
💬
ContextSnap™ Feedback
Smart feedback triggers that appear only at the moment of pain — not random popups. Context-aware questions every time.
🤖
InsightGPT™ Analysis
Powered by Groq + Llama 3. Reads all sessions and generates plain English pain point reports with prioritized fixes.
😊
Live Sentiment Engine
Analyzes feedback text in real-time as users type. Shows positive, neutral, or negative sentiment instantly.
🔴
Live Alert System
Real-time dashboard alerts when frustration is detected. Your team knows about critical UX issues instantly.
🔧
Auto Fix Suggestions
AI generates prioritized fix recommendations — High, Medium, Low priority. Your sprint backlog writes itself.
How it works

Up and running in
60 seconds

No complex setup. No SDK configuration. Just one script tag.

1

Add one script tag

Drop PulsePoint onto any website. It starts tracking immediately — no configuration needed.

<script src="pulsepoint.js"></script>
2

PulsePoint watches silently

Rage clicks, inactivity, hesitation — all detected automatically. A frustration score is calculated in real-time.

3

Smart feedback triggers at the right moment

When frustration score crosses the threshold, a contextual feedback widget appears — with a smart question based on what triggered it.

4

AI generates your fix report

One click on InsightGPT™ and Llama 3 reads all sessions, feedback, and behavior — then tells you exactly what to fix and in what order.
